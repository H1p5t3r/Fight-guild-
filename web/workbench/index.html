WORK ANYWHERE

Your moves. Your browser.

  1. Choose a fighter and move. Turn Attack enabled off to remove an unused command normal from play without deleting its data. Tune numbers, routes or hitbox dimensions.
  2. Use Art brief to describe poses and effects, and attach pose-reference photos with captions.
  3. Download JSON to keep a backup or move between PCs.
  4. At home, import it through the Godot move editor, or send the JSON back here for the next sprite pass.

Drafts are saved in this browser when storage is available. They do not sync between devices, and clearing browser data removes the local draft.

The page works offline without installing anything. It makes no network requests. The shared attack workspace runs approach and command inputs, contact, hitstop and target reactions. Pause or scrub its timeline, then drag hitboxes in Hit & movement or body boxes in Hurtboxes. Edits update the same scene and keep the selected move frame. Disabled or unmapped attacks show labelled, editable geometry without simulated contacts. Artwork uses existing poses; edits do not generate new animation frames.

In Hit & movement, add active-frame keys to change hitboxes over time. Multiple boxes count as one hit by default. Turn on Allow multiple hits and use different hit numbers for separate hits; boxes with the same number connect only once per attack.

Startup cannot go below 5 frames. Positions are in game pixels; speed is pixels per frame. Existing global rules and the other fighters’ data are preserved when you edit a move.

Pose photos and notes travel in the workshop JSON. Keep that file: Godot exports gameplay values only.
