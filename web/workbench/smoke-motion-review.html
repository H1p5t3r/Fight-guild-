What this audit checks
  • Readable load, contact and recovery silhouettes, with the largest strike movement at first active.
  • Fixed anatomy scale and deliberate held drawings; no blended poses or added body smears.
  • Effects aligned with authored reach and actual spawn time. Close/far pillars share the same artwork.
  • Real input, hit, block, whiff and hitstop recordings. A frame step advances one simulation tick.
  • Review at 1× before inspecting slow playback. Mirroring here checks artwork; automated tests exercise both actual facings.
