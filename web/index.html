6 + button invokes a command normal. 2 + P/K/S/Hs invokes a crouching normal. The initial specials are 236P, 236S, and 623Hs. Directions are relative to facing; 236 means down, down-forward, forward.

Pause + “1 frame” lets you inspect startup, active frames, and recovery. Blue boxes are hurtboxes; amber boxes are active hitboxes. Click the arena to restore keyboard control after editing. Online players both use Player 1 controls on their own computer.
