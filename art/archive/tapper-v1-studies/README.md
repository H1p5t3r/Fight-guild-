# Tapper rejected atlas layouts

These original image-generator outputs are kept for provenance. The first step attempt clipped its outer edge. Later review found neighboring hands, hooks or boots inside rectangular cells of the listed attack/step sheets. Revised generated sheets with wider transparent gutters are registered under `godot/assets/fighters/tapper-v1/`. Prompts and accepted hashes are recorded there in `generation.json` and `repairs.json`.

No image pixels were manually painted, erased or transformed. Runtime atlas crops and foot pivots are metadata; one windup uses a render crop to exclude a next-row hook while preserving its complete silhouette.
