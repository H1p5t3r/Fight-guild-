## Reading the motion

- Idle: rest/load, lift, shoulder contact, settle. Double Beat uses two smaller contacts and rebounds. The wrapped wooden handle contacts his shoulder; the metal hook remains clear.
- Walk and backward walk: contact and passing keys alternate support legs. Production needs additional contact/down/up poses and leg-identity verification at playback size.
- Backdash: load, push, retreat/flight, land and catch. The final study corrects the main sash lag toward the opponent during retreat; loose ends settle back as he stops.
- Run: alternating compression/contact and push/flight. These pose studies establish the attitude, torso angle and equipment carry; they are not complete four-frame production loops.
