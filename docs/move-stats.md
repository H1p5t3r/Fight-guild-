# Preliminary move data

Generated by `python scripts/generate-combat-data.py`. Edit `godot/data/starter_moves.json` for defaults, or use the in-game Move editor for local tuning. These are first-pass values, not final balance.

Startup minimums at 60 Hz: **2 frames for throws**, **3 for P normals** (normal or command), **5 for other attacks**. Standard ground and air throws start in 2 frames; command-grab timings remain individually authored. Active frame 1 is the startup-numbered tick. Block advantage below assumes first-active contact of the final hit; air/projectile advantage depends on contact timing. No invulnerability is implied by reversal eligibility.

Moves without dedicated signature art reuse the button strike rectangle. Fillet/Shark signatures use their own body/strike geometry; hooks use eight narrow moving rectangles along the spear, with a harmless rope. See [signature behavior and inputs](signature-moves.md). Weapons can deal damage but never extend hurtboxes. 2S is the universal low sweep with hard knockdown and punishable uncancelled block recovery; 4P+K and 6P+K are universal ground throws. These new slots reuse existing button art pending dedicated animation. Ground/air strike push is a distance budget in pixels, travelled over six action ticks and stopped by wall contact; block push is contact displacement. Launch is upward pixels per tick. KD: 0 none, 1 soft, 2 hard; down/wake durations are ticks.
